Pixel Patch: Changelog

What's changed recently, mostly fixes and tweaks from watching new players try the game for the first time.

Fix New / improved
August 20
  • Every crop now has its own leaf color while growing. A few pairs (Rubyfruit/Emberpod, Megamelon/Grandgourd, Suncup/Moonpetal) used to look completely identical until harvest, despite being different rarities.
  • The very first sprout you see after planting now hints at what's coming: a low leafy shoot, a tall spike, or a round bud, depending on the crop, instead of one generic sprout for all 22 crops.
  • Palmpearl, Wyrmfruit, Ambermango, Mochabean, Nectargem, Duskberry, Rubyfruit, and Emberpod each got their own unique plant shape. They used to share a silhouette with up to 5 other crops.
August 19
  • Brighter, more vibrant colors across the field and menus, in response to feedback that the game looked a little dull.
  • The field's dirt and grass tiles now have real texture and tile-to-tile variation instead of one flat color.
  • The Buyout Goal is now 3 escalating stages instead of one finish line. Save up enough and the buyer comes back with a bigger offer, so there's always another target to chase.
  • Made the shopkeepers easier to tell apart: bigger portraits with a backdrop that makes their faces stand out against the stand awnings.
August 18
  • Fixed the Continue button incorrectly showing up for a freshly created save slot.
August 17
  • Added two new faster-paced Upbeat music tracks, plus a Calm/Upbeat vibe selector in the menu.
  • Added a follow-up patience tip after claiming your first achievement, so it's clear your crop just needs a little time to grow.
  • Moved the Skip Tutorial button away from the shop stands so it can't be tapped by accident.
  • The tutorial no longer requires visiting the seed stand before it continues.
  • Fixed the Daily Reward popup covering up the tutorial's welcome tip on a brand-new farm.
August 16
  • The menu button now returns you to the main menu instead of closing outright, and you can switch farms without restarting the app.
August 15
  • NPC requests are now capped to crops the shop actually restocks reasonably often, so a request can't get stuck for hours waiting on a crop the shop almost never sells.
August 12
  • Background music now rotates between more tracks instead of looping the same one.
  • Richer, layered sound effects for harvesting, planting, watering, and buying.
  • Smoothed out early-game slow spots: quest rewards are capped to reasonable rarities, your first request from each NPC is always easy, and you start with one extra seed.
August 11
  • Added a one-time entrance animation the first time the title screen ever appears.
  • Fixed accidental menu taps caused by scroll gestures on touchscreens.
