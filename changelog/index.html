Pixel Patch: Changelog

What's changed in each update, from new features to the fixes that came from watching players try the game.

Fix New / improved
Version 1.3 · October 11
  • Pixel Patch now speaks Traditional Chinese. Every menu, shop, quest and line of dialogue is translated, and checked by a native speaker. Crop, gear and character names stay in English. Phones set to Chinese open the game in Chinese automatically, and you can switch any time from LANGUAGE / 語言 in the ☰ menu.
  • The title screen is now a pixel panorama of the Kaohsiung countryside, where Pixel Patch is made: green foothills, the grey mudstone ridges of Moon World, a red-brick farmhouse among betel-nut palms, a rice paddy and a sunflower field. It follows the real time of day, right down to lit windows at night.
  • The farm got the same treatment: rice paddies down both sides, palms on their banks, sunflowers by the Quest Board, and white egrets wading in the water during the day.
  • Two new music tracks: a samba in the Upbeat mix and a soft groove in the Calm one.
  • Tools you already own (Keepsake Tag, Reap-o-matic, Diggit, Peeper Lens) no longer show up in Greta's gear shop. Thanks to the player who suggested it!
  • A Credits screen at the bottom of the ☰ menu.
  • The cat that strolls across the farm about once an hour was walking behind the toolbar, so nobody had ever actually seen it. It now walks where you can see it. It still stays indoors when it rains.
  • With Action Messages set to Essential, the tips for the Diggit, Reap-o-matic and Keepsake Tag now still show, and so does "A sprinkler ran dry."
  • Fixed a faint click that could occasionally be heard at the start of a music note.
Version 1.2 · October
  • Messages no longer overwrite each other. Each one now waits its turn, so you get time to actually read it.
  • Tapping a stand opens its shop again. Only tapping the shopkeeper's face starts a chat.
  • Sell seeds you don't need back at Steena's stand, for half what you paid.
  • A Refresh Stock button at Sprout's stand rerolls the seed stock for a few coins, when you'd rather not wait for the next restock.
  • New Action Messages setting in the ☰ menu. Set it to Essential to hide the little "you just did this" confirmations and keep only warnings, rewards and tips.
  • Updated so the game keeps launching properly on the newest iOS versions.
Version 1.1 · September
  • New Wardrobe in the ☰ menu: pick the colors of your farmer's overalls, shirt and hat.
September 7
  • Pixel Patch launched on the App Store. Thank you to everyone who has planted a seed since.
August 24
  • The on-screen keyboard no longer shrinks the whole game while you type a farm or farmer name.
  • Fixed farm and farmer names getting lost, and stray popups appearing, after Save & Quit to Title.
August 22
  • Your save now backs up to iCloud automatically while you play. If you ever delete and reinstall the app, or move to a new phone, you'll be offered a "Restore" option for your farm instead of starting over from scratch.
  • Fixed an edge case where a save that failed to load properly could get silently overwritten with a blank farm. If this ever happens, you'll now see a clear warning instead of quietly losing progress.
  • Added optional notifications: your garden being ready to harvest, seed/gear shop restocks, your Daily Reward being available, and new quests, each with its own on/off switch plus a quiet hours window so nothing pings you overnight. Off by default, find it under the ☰ menu.
August 21
  • Claiming a quest reward now shows a clear confirmation (coins flying to your balance + a "Claimed!" toast) instead of just quietly updating your coin count in the background.
  • Fixed a rare case where a completed Daily or Weekly quest could become unclaimable if its refresh timer happened to pass while it was sitting there waiting to be claimed.
  • Each shopkeeper now has their own small hand-drawn portrait out in the field, not just a shrunk copy of their dialogue art, so Sprout, Greta, and Steena are each unmistakable at a glance.
  • You can now give your farmer a name when starting a new save slot. It shows up on your Statistics profile and the title screen greets you by name.
  • Starting a fresh farm now asks if you'd like to keep or change your farmer's name first, instead of silently resetting it.
  • Fixed a bug where starting your first farm from the title screen could quietly reset the farm and farmer names you'd just entered moments earlier.
August 20
  • Every crop now has its own leaf color while growing. A few pairs (Rubyfruit/Emberpod, Megamelon/Grandgourd, Suncup/Moonpetal) used to look completely identical until harvest, despite being different rarities.
  • The very first sprout you see after planting now hints at what's coming: a low leafy shoot, a tall spike, or a round bud, depending on the crop, instead of one generic sprout for all 22 crops.
  • Palmpearl, Wyrmfruit, Ambermango, Mochabean, Nectargem, Duskberry, Rubyfruit, and Emberpod each got their own unique plant shape. They used to share a silhouette with up to 5 other crops.
  • Tutorial tip messages now wait for you to tap them before disappearing, instead of vanishing on a timer, so there's time to actually read them.
  • Fixed a tutorial tip disappearing early if you took another action (like planting a second seed) before reading it.
  • Fixed rapid tapping across several ready crops sometimes silently skipping some of them, so nothing you tap gets missed.
August 19
  • Brighter, more vibrant colors across the field and menus, in response to feedback that the game looked a little dull.
  • The field's dirt and grass tiles now have real texture and tile-to-tile variation instead of one flat color.
  • The Buyout Goal is now 3 escalating stages instead of one finish line. Save up enough and the buyer comes back with a bigger offer, so there's always another target to chase.
  • Made the shopkeepers easier to tell apart: bigger portraits with a backdrop that makes their faces stand out against the stand awnings.
August 18
  • Fixed the Continue button incorrectly showing up for a freshly created save slot.
August 17
  • Added two new faster-paced Upbeat music tracks, plus a Calm/Upbeat vibe selector in the menu.
  • Added a follow-up patience tip after claiming your first achievement, so it's clear your crop just needs a little time to grow.
  • Moved the Skip Tutorial button away from the shop stands so it can't be tapped by accident.
  • The tutorial no longer requires visiting the seed stand before it continues.
  • Fixed the Daily Reward popup covering up the tutorial's welcome tip on a brand-new farm.
August 16
  • The menu button now returns you to the main menu instead of closing outright, and you can switch farms without restarting the app.
August 15
  • NPC requests are now capped to crops the shop actually restocks reasonably often, so a request can't get stuck for hours waiting on a crop the shop almost never sells.
August 12
  • Background music now rotates between more tracks instead of looping the same one.
  • Richer, layered sound effects for harvesting, planting, watering, and buying.
  • Smoothed out early-game slow spots: quest rewards are capped to reasonable rarities, your first request from each NPC is always easy, and you start with one extra seed.
August 11
  • Added a one-time entrance animation the first time the title screen ever appears.
  • Fixed accidental menu taps caused by scroll gestures on touchscreens.
