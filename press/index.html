Boilerplate

Copy & paste Pixel Patch is a cozy, pixel-art farming game for iOS built entirely solo: every sprite hand-drawn, every sound effect synthesized in code, no external art or audio assets anywhere in the game. Plant, water, harvest, and sell at the market stand across 22 crops and 7 rarity tiers, discover rare crop mutations, build friendships with the market's NPCs, and grow a farm that keeps progressing even while you're away. No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection. Just a farm.

Key Features

22 crops, 7 rarity tiers
From everyday vegetables to rare mutations: Golden, Rainbow, Diamond, and beyond.
Fully hand-made
Every sprite is hand-drawn pixel art and every sound is synthesized in code, with no stock art or audio anywhere in the game.
Your farm keeps growing
Offline growth means crops progress even while you're away, so you can check in whenever you like.
A living village
Three shopkeeper NPCs with their own personalities, dialogue, and a friendship system tied to daily requests.
No ads, no IAP, no data
One-time purchase. No ads, no in-app purchases, and no data collection of any kind, ever.
Treasure & seasonal events
Hidden treasure digging, a 22-entry collection book, achievements, and rotating seasonal decor.

About the Developer

Pixel Patch is built and maintained by Crian Padayachee, a single solo developer working nights and weekends. Every line of code, every pixel, and every sound effect is original work: no asset packs, no outsourcing, no team. This is a first solo release, made because a cozy game that respects your time and never asks for money after the initial purchase felt worth building.
