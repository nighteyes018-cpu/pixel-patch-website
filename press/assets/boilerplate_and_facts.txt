BOILERPLATE (copy/paste into articles)

Pixel Patch is a cozy, pixel-art farming game for iOS built entirely solo:
every sprite hand-drawn, every sound effect synthesized in code, no external
art or audio assets anywhere in the game. Plant, water, harvest, and sell at
the market stand across 22 crops and 7 rarity tiers, discover rare crop
mutations, build friendships with the market's NPCs, and grow a farm that
keeps progressing even while you're away. No ads, no in-app purchases, no
data collection. Just a farm.

SHORT PITCH (one-liner)

A cozy, pixel-art farming game built entirely solo. No ads, no in-app
purchases, just a farm that grows in real time.

KEY FEATURES

- 22 crops across 7 rarity tiers, with rare mutations (Golden, Rainbow,
  Diamond, and beyond)
- Every sprite hand-drawn, every sound synthesized in code, with no stock
  assets anywhere
- Offline growth: crops progress even while the app is closed
- Three shopkeeper NPCs with their own personalities and a friendship system
- Treasure digging, a 22-entry collection book, and achievements
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection

ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

Pixel Patch is built and maintained by Crian Padayachee, a single solo
developer working nights and weekends. Every line of code, every pixel, and
every sound effect is original work: no asset packs, no outsourcing, no
team. This is a first solo release.
