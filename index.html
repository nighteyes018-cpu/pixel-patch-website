Pixel Patch is a cozy, pixel-art farming game. Plant seeds, water them, watch them grow, and sell your harvest at the market, all at your own pace, with no pressure and nothing trying to grab your attention when you're not playing.

What's inside

🌱 22 crops, 7 rarity tiers
From everyday vegetables to rare mutations: Golden, Rainbow, Diamond, and beyond.
🎨 Fully hand-made
Every sprite is hand-drawn pixel art and every sound is synthesized in code, with no stock art or audio anywhere in the game.
🌤️ Your farm keeps growing
Offline growth means your crops progress even while you're away, so check in whenever you like.
🔒 No ads, no IAP, no data
One-time purchase. No ads, no in-app purchases, and no data collection of any kind, ever.
